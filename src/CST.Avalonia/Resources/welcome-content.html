🧪 Beta Testing

Thank you for testing CST Reader! Your feedback will help shape the final release.

Focus Areas

Beta 5 is the first release at feature parity with CST4. These areas are new or changed, so feedback on them is especially valuable:

Windows — brand new in this release
  • Install and first launch: does the text download and index build complete?
  • Everyday reading, searching and dictionary lookups
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+O, Ctrl+G, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+F, Ctrl+P, Ctrl+, for Settings
  • If book text appears blank, turn off hardware acceleration in Settings and restart

This is the least-tested part of the release. Anything at all that looks wrong is worth reporting.

Dictionaries
  • Switch between sources with the picker: the VRI dictionaries, the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary, and the Dictionary of Pāḷi Proper Names
  • Choose which dictionaries appear, and in what order, under Settings › Dictionary
  • The extra dictionaries download on first run — did that complete without trouble?
Printing
  • Print a whole book, and print a selection (⌘P / Ctrl+P, ⇧⌘P / Ctrl+Shift+P)
  • Check that search highlights remain legible on paper
Windows and tabs
  • Float a book by dragging its tab out of the main window, and drag it back to re-dock (the old Float and Dock buttons are gone)
  • Drag the book tree and search panel out and back — watch for the drop-target indicators
  • Reorder book tabs, and split the reading area

Does your reading position survive floating, re-docking and restarting?

🚀 Quick Start

  • Open books: In the Select a Book tab, double-click a text — or select it and press Enter
  • Change script: Use the dropdown at the top (applies to all open books)
  • Search: Open the Search tab in the left panel (or View › Search), enter Pāli text, and optionally filter by book categories
  • Dictionary: Select a word and press ⌘D / Ctrl+D
  • Settings: Choose fonts for each script, and which dictionaries to use

Known Limitations

  • The texts are presented in Pāli only — CST Reader does not include translations. The built-in dictionaries give the meaning of individual words.
  • Menus and dialogs are English only. CST4 offers 24 interface languages; that work is still ahead.
  • Book content fonts not yet customizable (UI fonts for each script are customizable in Settings)
  • macOS: Elevated CPU usage (~30% idle) is an Avalonia framework limitation, not specific to CST Reader

About the Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana Tipiṭaka

The texts in this application come from the Sixth Council (Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana) held in Myanmar from 1954-1956. This council, commemorating 2,500 years since the Buddha's Parinibbāna, produced the most authoritative edition of the Pāli Canon through careful comparison of manuscripts from Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Thailand, and Cambodia.

The texts have been carefully digitized and made available by the Vipassana Research Institute. We are deeply grateful to the scholars, editors, and meditators who participated in this monumental undertaking of preserving and sharing these teachings.

CST Reader provides digital access to all 217 texts from this edition, maintaining the authentic Pāli while offering modern tools for study and research.
